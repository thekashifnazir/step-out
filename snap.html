Most local life never makes it online — it lives on chalkboards, flyers and PDF newsletters. That messy middle is a language-model job: drop a photo or a PDF and watch board entries fall out, ready to join your board.

📸 Snap it onto the board

A photo of a noticeboard or chalkboard (JPEG/PNG/HEIC where your browser can read it), or a PDF newsletter. Photos are downscaled in your browser before anything is sent. The first read of the day takes longest — the extraction schema compiles once, then it's cached.
