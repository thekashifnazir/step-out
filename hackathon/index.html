Step Out

What's going on around you, matched to what you actually asked for.

Local life lives in PDF newsletters, WhatsApp groups and chalkboards, invisible unless someone tells you. Step Out puts it on one board, matches it to a plain sentence about your situation, and notices what people keep asking for that doesn't exist yet.

▶ Start the two-minute tour or start at the board →

The two-minute tour

Three things to see, in order. Each step runs its example for you, and a floating chip carries you to the next.

1

Ask it

Open the board and tell it your situation, like a neighbour would: “I've just moved here and barely know anyone, I'd love something regular near me.” Ranked matches come back, each with a specific reason, and it answers in your language: tested in Bengali and Somali, the languages of the venue's own neighbourhood.

Start at the board → 2

Catch what's missing

Now ask for “a running group, mornings, before work.” There isn't one. Instead of a dead end you get a demand card: widen, connect, create, inform. The engine behind it lives on the Demand page.

Fire the demand card → 3

Feed it

Drop a photo of a noticeboard, or a whole PDF newsletter, onto Snap. Structured entries fall out, with personal contact details stripped to roles and anything date-stale flagged for re-checking.
